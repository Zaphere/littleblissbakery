You are a senior full-stack engineer and UI/UX designer.

Your task is to design and build a complete, production-quality MVP application called:

LITTLE BLISS BAKERY
Bakery Management, Costing & Profit System

IMPORTANT:
This is a real operational tool for a small bakery in Eswatini. The application must prioritize simplicity, accuracy, and daily usability.

Do not over-engineer the application.

Do NOT use Supabase.
Do NOT require a cloud database.
Do NOT require paid services.
Do NOT build unnecessary authentication for the initial version.

The application should work as a local-first system that can run on a laptop and store its data locally.

==================================================
1. PRIMARY BUSINESS PURPOSE
==================================================

The application must help Little Bliss Bakery understand:

1. How much ingredients cost.
2. How much it costs to produce each product.
3. How much each individual item costs to make.
4. How much each dozen costs to produce.
5. How much revenue each order generates.
6. How much profit is expected from each order.
7. How much money was actually received.
8. What bakery expenses were paid.
9. How much money is available after expenses.
10. How much profit the bakery has generated over time.
11. Which products are the most profitable.
12. How ingredient price changes affect product profitability.

The bakery owner must be able to easily edit ANY price, recipe, product, order, or expense.

This must not be a complicated accounting system. It should feel like a simple business dashboard designed specifically for Little Bliss Bakery.

Structure the application so that a future PostgreSQL or backend integration could be added later, but DO NOT build that backend now.
